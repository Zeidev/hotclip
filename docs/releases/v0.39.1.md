# HotClip v0.39.1 — 切点按真音频落,爆点闪现按持续能量挑

## 问题在哪

上一版把「时间戳口径」修齐了,但**切点本身**还建立在估出来的词时间上,而**闪现闪的
是哪一刻**几乎等于随机:

- SRT 导入把词时间按比例铺开、VAD 兜底时也只是估的。冷开场迷你片两端、强制剪除
  区间(口头语/重拍/拼接)都吸附到这些假词边上——两层保险同时在盲切。真实素材上
  成片头落在句子中间 280ms 处,正片尾在 452.78s 切掉真人声(真语音到 452.85s)。
- 爆点闪现按**单块峰值**倒序挑。实测同一条片里六个事件的最响块是 0.536–0.566,
  差 2%——排序等于掷骰子;而且地板是 `0.75 × 全局最高块`,一下 33ms 的碰麦尖峰
  就能把整段 2.5 秒的大笑抬到地板之下,**整段爆发从事件表里消失**。

## 切点落在真静音里(`audio-peaks.ts` / `cut.ts` / `gaps.ts` / `qa.ts` / `export.ts`)

- `audio-peaks` 新增按音频量切点的四个工具:`quietRunAt`(静音 = 连续 0.06s 且
  峰值 ≤0.04;单独一块 33ms 低值常常只是塞音闭合,不算字间)、`snapSpansToQuiet`
  (强制剪除区间的两端各挪到附近谷底,空区间直接丢掉)、`relativeQuietPeak`
  (密集口语里没有绝对静音,门槛按**这一段自己的电平**定,带上下限)。
- `snapEdgeToQuiet`:整条成片的头/尾是**真切点**了——只许往外挪(只会多留声音,
  不会把已经算好的边界往里收),`manualBounds` 一律不动。
- `buildJumpCutArgs` 下调快速定位,首段被带到 `clipStartSec` 之前时不再被静默丢掉。
- `countMidWordCuts` 有峰值轨就信音频:两侧都在响才算切在词中间——顺带干掉一批
  由估词边界产生的假告警。
- 真素材复核:冷开场头 437.911 → 437.544、冷开场尾 440.635 → 440.902、正片尾
  452.780 → 452.880,三处都落在静音段里。

## 爆点闪现:按持续能量挑那一刻(`audio-peaks.ts` / `coldopen.ts` / `export.ts`)

- `findPeakEvents` 改按**持续能量**排序(`score = 该段块均值 × 时长`,时长封顶 2s),
  地板由 `0.75 × p98(块峰值)` 给出并保底 `0.25 × 最高块`:一下 33ms 的爆点再也
  压不过 2.5 秒的大笑,也再也不能把整段爆发抬出事件表。中心点取最响那一带的**重心**,
  不再是随机某一块。
- `flashWindow` 按事件的**响区间**建窗(而不是 ±固定 lead/tail 截半句话),吸附到词
  边界后仍不许超 `FLASH_MAX_SEC`:超了就从头部整词丢,**保住爆点**。
- `planFlashForward` 按分数选,分数在 15% 以内时**挑成片里更靠后的那个**——闪现是
  预告结局,不是把开头再演一遍。
- 迷你片两端加 `skipIfOnset` + `landing:"speech-edge"`:词头/词尾本身就该切在那里,
  再往静音里退只会把整段停顿的死气带进开场;落到长停顿深处时贴到发声边上,只留
  约 0.06s 引子(实测句间停顿 0.43s)。
- `isSpeechEdge` 改为按**块号**取样:`peakInRange` 的窗口末端是 `ceil`,窗口收到
  `atSec` 之前时仍会把含 `atSec` 的那一块算进来——干净的发声交界被自己判成「一直在响」。
- 真素材对照:旧选 437.916–440.880(2.96s,从断句「artık şey olmayacak.」开始),
  新选 439.360–441.731(2.37s,「Discord 'la falan uğraşmayacağız. Kendimiz」),
  静态吸附后 439.027–441.731(2.70s,比人声早约 0.06s 起)。全片能量最高的一刻
  (score 0.826,落在成片 1.5s)按 6 秒守卫照旧不闪——那是观众 1 秒后马上要看的内容。

## English

**Why**: the previous release made the timestamp accounting agree with what ships,
but the cut points themselves were still placed on *estimated* word times — SRT
import spreads word times proportionally, and the VAD fallback is an estimate
too, so the cold-open window edges and the force-removed spans (filler, retake,
stitch) were snapped to fabricated boundaries with both safety layers blind. On
a real VOD the export head sat 280 ms inside a phrase and the body tail cut
speech at 452.78 s while real voice ran to 452.85 s. The flash-forward was
picked by single-block peak, and the six events of one real clip spanned
0.536–0.566 — a 2% spread, so the ordering was effectively random — while an
`0.75 × global max` floor let one 33 ms transient hide a 2.5 s burst completely.

**Fixes**: `audio-peaks` gains `quietRunAt` (a quiet run is 0.06 s at ≤ 0.04
peak), `snapSpansToQuiet`, `snapEdgeToQuiet` and `relativeQuietPeak`; whole-clip
edges are real cut points that may only move outward, and `manualBounds` are
never touched. `buildJumpCutArgs` lowers the fast seek so a first segment pulled
before `clipStartSec` is no longer dropped, and `countMidWordCuts` trusts peaks
when it has them (both sides must sound). Peak events now rank by sustained
energy (run mean × duration, capped at 2 s) over a p98 floor with a `0.25 × max`
minimum, windows are built from the event's loud run, `FLASH_MAX_SEC` is
enforced by dropping leading whole words so the payoff survives, and scores
within 15% prefer the later output position. Mini-clip edges use `skipIfOnset`
plus `landing:"speech-edge"` (a clean word boundary is left alone, a long pause
keeps ~0.06 s of lead-in), and `isSpeechEdge` probes by block index because
`peakInRange`'s ceil-rounded window pulled the onset block into its "before"
probe. On the same clip the flash pick moves from 437.916–440.880 (2.96 s,
starting mid-sentence on a filler) to 439.360–441.731 (2.37 s); the clip's
loudest moment still is not flashed, by design — it sits at output 1.5 s, inside
the 6 s guard.

**Known gaps**: the `cover-quality.integration` failure is a local FFmpeg
environment issue (none of its dependencies changed); the new flash pick is
covered by unit tests and an offline real-VOD probe, not by a real export of the
opening seconds; no prompt changed, so no live-endpoint rerun.

**Validation**: both tsconfigs typecheck clean; 1,673 tests pass, 1 skipped, and
the single failure is the environment item above.
